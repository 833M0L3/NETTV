# Getting Started

![LOGO](/assets/nettvlogo.png)

Since NETTV launched its IPTV service in 2016, it has dominated the Nepali television market. With its advanced features and capabilities, NETTV changed how people experience television, making traditional viewing feel outdated. Over the years, the NETTV brand has gone through several transformations. Today, it is owned by WorldLink Communications, an ISP known for its restrictive approach, including limited router access for customers. Similar restrictions also apply to the NETTV set-top boxes they provide.

Since 2016, NETTV has used various generations of set-top boxes, each updated and redesigned over time. This site aims to document every NETTV set-top box used in the past and present.

The main purpose of this site is to offer clear, useful information about NETTV’s existing set-top boxes and how they are used.
